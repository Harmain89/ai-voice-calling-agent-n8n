AI Voice Calling Agent | Real Estate Automation | Vapi + n8n + OpenAI


Built a fully automated AI voice calling agent for a real estate 
business that handles inbound calls 24/7 without any human involvement. 
The AI assistant answers calls, understands the caller's intent, searches 
a live Google Sheets property database for details like price, bedrooms, 
and square footage, and delivers instant answers through natural voice 
conversation — powered by Vapi, OpenAI GPT-4.1-mini, and Deepgram 
speech recognition.

When a caller wants to book a viewing, the agent collects their name, 
email, phone number, and preferred time through conversation, 
automatically creates a Google Calendar event with full attendee details, 
and sends an instant WhatsApp notification to the real estate agent — all 
in a single call. The entire backend is built on n8n workflow automation, 
ensuring fast response times under 2 seconds, a call cost of ~$0.11/min, 
and zero manual work required after setup.
